The Region

Administrative & Agrarian Heritage

Nestled within the Palakkad district of Kerala, Polpully is a serene Grama Panchayath characterized by its sweeping agricultural expanses, undulating terrain, and vibrant rural communities.

Serving as a vital administrative unit, the region perfectly balances traditional agrarian livelihoods with evolving local self-governance. Understanding its geography—from land use patterns to settlement distribution—is essential for sustainable local-level planning.
